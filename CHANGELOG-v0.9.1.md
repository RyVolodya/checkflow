# CheckFlow v0.9.1

- Added visible completion timestamps for completed tasks.
- Added completion timestamp for every completed checklist item.
- Checklist card now shows the most recently completed checklist item and its completion time.
- Completing the final checklist item closes the whole checklist immediately.
- Checklist `completedAt` now uses the timestamp of the last completed item.
- Re-clicking an already completed checklist item no longer rewrites its original completion time.
- Completed task/checklist details show actual completion time and elapsed duration from work start.
- Based on v0.9.0; the previously discarded v0.9.1 efficiency patch is not included.
