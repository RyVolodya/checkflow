# CheckFlow v0.10.1

- Added permanent task/checklist deletion for Administrators only.
- Deleting a work item also removes its related database records and uploaded checklist photos.
- Refined Kanban cards to use a neutral CRM-style appearance instead of inheriting column/status-colored card outlines.
- Added live drag-and-drop destination preview when hovering a card over another Kanban column.
- Kanban column counters preview the pending card placement while dragging.
- Dropping a card applies the status move immediately; overdue items moved back to In Work still require new start/due dates.
